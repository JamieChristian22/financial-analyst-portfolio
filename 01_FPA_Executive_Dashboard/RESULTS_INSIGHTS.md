# Executive Results & Management Insights

## Executive Summary
The FY2025 synthetic business generated approximately **$11.26M in revenue**, **57.2% gross margin**, and approximately **$618K in operating profit**, producing a positive operating margin. Revenue finished modestly above plan, while operating expenses were above plan and compressed profit versus budget.

## Primary Management Takeaways
### 1. Revenue is broadly on plan
Revenue is slightly favorable to budget, which means the main management issue is not top-line underperformance.

### 2. Gross margin is below plan
Gross margin finished below the planned level, indicating some pressure from COGS and mix. Management should monitor supplier costs, pricing, and product/customer mix.

### 3. Operating expenses are the main profitability pressure
OpEx is above plan, with the most relevant attention areas in payroll, software, travel, and discretionary departmental spending.

### 4. Operating profit is positive but below budget
The company remains profitable, but the profit shortfall shows why FP&A must connect variance analysis to management actions rather than only report results.

### 5. Forecast governance matters
The FY2026 forecast uses centralized assumptions and links every major planning driver to forecast output. Material changes should be logged and approved.

### 6. Liquidity remains adequate
The cash forecast maintains liquidity above the modeled minimum-cash threshold, providing a management view of cash sustainability.

## Recommended Management Actions
- Require business-owner commentary for absolute variances ≥5%.
- Review R&D payroll and software commitments monthly.
- Reforecast travel and discretionary spend each month.
- Maintain headcount-to-plan reporting by department.
- Reconcile dashboard KPIs to the Excel model before publishing.
- Review forecast assumptions with business partners at each monthly cycle.
- Compare prior forecast to actual results to identify recurring bias.
- Track cash against a minimum-liquidity threshold.
