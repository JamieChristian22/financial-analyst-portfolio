# FP&A Case Study

## Situation
Leadership needs a repeatable monthly process to understand whether the business is tracking to plan and where corrective action is required.

## Task
Build a corporate-style finance workflow that combines actuals, budget, forecasting, scenario planning, departmental analysis, liquidity, and management reporting.

## Analysis
The project:
- validates a 420-row financial dataset,
- produces a monthly P&L,
- compares actuals with plan,
- flags material variances,
- identifies expense drivers,
- builds an FY2026 forecast,
- evaluates upside/downside scenarios,
- models headcount/payroll,
- forecasts free cash flow and liquidity,
- and summarizes executive actions.

## Result
The model shows a profitable synthetic company with revenue broadly on plan but operating profit below budget due to expense and margin pressure. The resulting recommendations focus on cost governance, headcount controls, rolling reforecasting, and management accountability.

## Business Value
The project demonstrates how FP&A converts accounting/operational data into decisions rather than simply producing reports.
