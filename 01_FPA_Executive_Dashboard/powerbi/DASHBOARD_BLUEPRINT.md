# Power BI Executive Dashboard Blueprint

## Page 1 — Executive Overview
Top KPI cards:
- Revenue
- Gross Margin %
- Total OpEx
- Operating Profit
- Operating Margin %

Visuals:
- Monthly Revenue & Operating Profit trend
- Department OpEx Budget vs Actual
- Material variance table
- Executive management commentary

## Page 2 — P&L & Margin Trend
- Revenue, COGS, Gross Profit
- Gross Margin %
- Operating Profit
- Operating Margin %
- Month-over-month trend

## Page 3 — Department Variance
- Department variance waterfall / bar
- Budget vs Actual
- Variance $ and %
- Materiality status
- drill-through to account detail

## Page 4 — Account Drivers
- Payroll
- Software
- Travel
- Facilities
- Other OpEx
- ranked unfavorable/favorable drivers

## Page 5 — Forecast & Scenario
- FY2025 actual vs FY2026 forecast
- Base / Upside / Downside
- forecast bridge
- sensitivity matrix summary

## Page 6 — Headcount & Cash
- planned headcount
- payroll impact
- monthly ending cash
- minimum cash threshold
- liquidity status

## Page 7 — Commentary & Controls
- variance commentary
- action owner
- cadence
- control completion status

## UX Standards
- Keep executive page to one screen.
- Use consistent fiscal period slicers.
- Use red only for exceptions/unfavorable items.
- Use tooltips for KPI definitions.
- Include Last Refresh timestamp.
- Add a “Definitions” info page or tooltip.
