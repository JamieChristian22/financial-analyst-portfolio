# Amazon Marketplace Analytics — Corporate Case Study

## Situation
Marketplace leadership needs to know whether growth creates profitable economics, which sellers/categories deserve investment, and where refunds or variable costs erode value.

## Scope
1,965 synthetic orders, 80 sellers, seller tiers, regions, and multiple product categories.

## Results
- GMV: $10,220,324
- Marketplace Revenue: $1,333,703
- Take Rate: 13.0%
- Refund Rate: 4.9%
- Contribution Margin: $895,646
- Contribution Margin %: 67.2%
- AOV: $5,201

## Decisions
Protect top sellers, develop high-potential tiers, prioritize categories with strong margin/low returns, investigate high-return categories, and allocate growth spend using contribution efficiency rather than GMV alone.
