# Monthly Marketplace Business Review (MBR) Playbook

## Pre-Close
1. Validate transaction completeness and duplicate OrderID.
2. Confirm seller/category dimension mappings.
3. Reconcile transaction GMV and marketplace revenue.
4. Review refund exceptions and late adjustments.

## Performance Review
1. Compare GMV, revenue, take rate, refund rate, contribution margin, and operating profit to plan.
2. Review seller-tier and top-seller concentration.
3. Review category profitability and opportunity score.
4. Review promotion efficiency and fulfillment economics.
5. Identify material drivers, not only variances.

## Forecast Update
Update GMV growth, take rate, refunds, promotional spend, support/fulfillment costs, and fixed platform costs only when supported by a documented business driver.

## Management Actions
Each material exception should have:
- owner
- financial impact
- root cause
- action
- due date
- next-review status

## Publication Control
Finance/analytics should reconcile Excel and BI totals before publishing the MBR.
