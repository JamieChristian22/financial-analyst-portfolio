# Recruiter One-Page Project Summary

**Project:** Amazon Marketplace Analytics  
**Role alignment:** Financial Analyst | Business Analyst | BI Analyst | Data Analyst | Marketplace / E-commerce Analyst

**Scope:** 1,965 synthetic marketplace orders, 80 sellers, 6 categories, FY2025.

**Tools:** Excel, SQL, Python, Power BI/DAX, Tableau-ready calculations.

**Business analysis:** GMV, marketplace revenue, take rate, AOV, refunds, contribution margin, seller performance, category profitability, promotion efficiency, fulfillment economics, working capital, forecast scenarios.

**Corporate deliverables:** Executive dashboard, KPI scorecard, monthly marketplace P&L, seller/category scorecards, forecast, scenario model, management commentary, controls, data dictionary, reconciliation checklist.

**Executive result:** approximately $10.2M modeled GMV, $1.33M marketplace revenue, $896K contribution margin, and $476K operating profit.

**Decision impact:** recommends seller retention/development, refund root-cause analysis, category-level capital allocation, contribution-based promotional budgeting, and scenario-driven forecasting.

**Disclosure:** All data and assumptions are synthetic. This project is not affiliated with Amazon.
