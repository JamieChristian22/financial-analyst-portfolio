# Corporate Case Study
## Situation
Management needs to understand why gross margin changes and which commercial/cost levers can improve profitability.
## Task
Connect product/channel revenue, discounting, and COGS components to gross profit and management action.
## Analysis
The upgraded case covers revenue realization, product/channel margin, COGS structure, discount leakage, PVM thinking, inflation risk, forecasting, and sensitivity analysis.
## Result
A management-ready framework that translates margin movement into pricing, sourcing, mix, and cost-control actions.
