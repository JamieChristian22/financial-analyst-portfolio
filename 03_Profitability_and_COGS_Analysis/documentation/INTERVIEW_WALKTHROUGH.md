# Interview Walkthrough
## 60-second pitch
I built a corporate-style profitability and COGS analysis to explain not only what gross margin is, but what drives it. I analyzed product and channel profitability, discount leakage, material, labor, freight and overhead costs, price-volume-mix logic, cost inflation, forecasts and scenarios. I used Excel modeling concepts, SQL for KPI analysis, Python for validation, and Power BI/DAX for executive reporting. The project converts margin changes into pricing, sourcing, mix and cost-control recommendations.

## Be ready to explain
Net vs list revenue; COGS; gross profit; gross margin; discount leakage; product/channel mix; price-volume-mix; purchase-price/cost variance; scenario and sensitivity analysis.

## Advanced Defense
Be ready to explain PPV, usage variance, price/volume/mix, gross-profit bridges, margin exceptions, and why a bridge must reconcile beginning to ending gross profit.
