# Power BI / Tableau Executive Dashboard Build Specification

## Page 1 — Executive Summary
KPI cards:
- Revenue
- Revenue vs Budget %
- Revenue YoY %
- Gross Margin
- EBITDA
- EBITDA Margin
- Operating Cash Flow
- Cash Conversion

Visuals:
- Monthly Actual vs Budget Revenue line chart
- EBITDA and EBITDA Margin trend
- Business-unit revenue variance bar chart
- Management risk / action table

Slicers:
- Year
- Quarter
- Business Unit
- Region

## Page 2 — Variance Analysis
- Actual vs Budget waterfall
- Revenue variance by business unit
- Gross-margin variance
- Opex variance
- Top favorable / unfavorable drivers

## Page 3 — Business Unit Performance
- Revenue, EBITDA, and margin by business unit
- Region heatmap
- Revenue mix
- Headcount productivity

## Page 4 — Forecast & Scenario
- Actual + forecast monthly trend
- Base / Upside / Downside FY revenue and EBITDA
- Forecast accuracy trend
- Assumption summary

## Design Standard
- Use one executive color family.
- Keep KPI cards at the top.
- Display units ($M / %) clearly.
- Use red only for unfavorable exceptions.
- Avoid overcrowding.
- Every chart must answer a management question.
