# Monthly FP&A Close & Management Reporting Playbook

## Day 1–2: Data Intake and Validation
- Load actual revenue, COGS, Opex, assets, headcount, and operating cash flow.
- Confirm reporting period completeness.
- Check duplicate records, nulls, sign conventions, and business-unit mapping.
- Reconcile source totals to the accounting / reporting source of truth.

## Day 2–3: Variance Analysis
- Calculate Actual vs Budget and Actual vs Forecast.
- Review both dollar and percentage variance.
- Rank material variances by financial impact.
- Separate timing differences from structural issues.
- Identify volume, price, mix, direct-cost, and spend drivers.

## Day 3–4: Forecast Update
- Update revenue growth, gross-margin, and Opex assumptions.
- Reforecast remaining periods.
- Compare latest estimate to budget and previous forecast.
- Quantify forecast bias and accuracy.
- Run base, upside, and downside scenarios.

## Day 4: Business Partner Review
- Meet with business-unit owners.
- Validate root causes.
- Confirm corrective actions, owners, and timing.
- Capture risks and opportunities.

## Day 5: Executive Package
- Publish scorecard.
- Present top favorable and unfavorable variances.
- Highlight forecast changes.
- Summarize decisions required from leadership.
- Archive controls and reconciliation evidence.

## Materiality Guideline
Investigate a variance when it exceeds the larger of:
- 2% of budget for the KPI; or
- a management-defined dollar threshold.

## Commentary Standard
Avoid: “Revenue was below plan.”
Use: “SMB revenue finished 3.2% below plan due to lower renewal volume in the East region; management action is targeted retention outreach and revised Q4 conversion assumptions.”
