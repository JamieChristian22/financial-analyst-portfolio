# Financial Analytics Portfolio (FP&A, Reporting & Decision Support)

Welcome to my **Financial Analyst portfolio** focused on **FP&A workflows, financial performance reporting, and executive decision support**.

This folder contains job-ready financial analytics projects demonstrating real-world skills used by Financial Analysts, FP&A Analysts, and Business Finance teams.

---

## What this portfolio demonstrates

- **Budget vs Actual analysis** (variance, drivers, bridges)
- **Forecasting & trend analysis** (MoM / YoY performance)
- **Profitability & margin analysis** (COGS, contribution margin)
- **Financial performance reporting** (executive KPIs)
- **Scenario modeling & ROI evaluation**
- **Excel financial models** (structured, realistic workbooks)
- **SQL & Python analysis** supporting financial insights
- **Power BI / Tableau dashboards** designed for leadership audiences

> Any datasets labeled *simulated* are intentionally structured to mirror real company finance systems for portfolio purposes.

---

## Projects in this folder

- **01_FPA_Executive_Dashboard**  
  Executive-level financial performance dashboard with KPIs, trends, and variance analysis.

- **02_Amazon_Marketplace_Analytics**  
  Financial performance analysis of a marketplace business model (Revenue, margin, seller performance, category profitability).
