# Delivery Cadence (Agile-Friendly)

## Weekly
- Delivery sync (30 min): blockers, dependencies, risk updates
- Architecture review (45 min): decisions, tradeoffs, upcoming changes

## Bi-Weekly
- Sprint planning (60 min): commit scope + acceptance criteria
- Demo (30–45 min): show progress to stakeholders
- Retro (30 min): improve process + reduce friction

## Decision Hygiene
- Record major design choices in ADRs
- Track action items in RAID log with owners + due dates
