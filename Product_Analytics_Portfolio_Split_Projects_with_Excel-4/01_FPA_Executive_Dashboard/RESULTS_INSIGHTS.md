# Results & Insights — FP&A Executive Dashboard

## What changed (findings)
- Revenue outperformed budget in several months while Travel showed the highest volatility vs plan.
- Operations and R&D payroll variance drove the majority of OpEx variance in the second half of the year.
- Gross profit variability tracked revenue swings more than COGS rate changes.

## What I recommend (decisions)
- Implement department-level monthly variance reviews with thresholds (±10%) and owner actions.
- Re-forecast Travel quarterly and require pre-approval for spend above baseline.
- Add hiring-plan guardrails (headcount + salary band) to stabilize payroll variance.

## Next steps

- Automate refresh (scheduled data pulls + semantic model).
- Add anomaly detection thresholds for variance investigation.
