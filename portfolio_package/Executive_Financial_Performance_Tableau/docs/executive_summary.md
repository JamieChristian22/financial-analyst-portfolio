# Executive Summary - Executive Financial Performance

## What leadership can answer with this dashboard
- Are sales and profit trending up over time? Which products and segments are driving changes?
- How effective are discounts (do they raise volume without destroying margin)?
- Is liquidity healthy (Current + Quick ratios)? Are we becoming more leveraged (Debt-to-Equity)?

## KPI definitions
- Gross Sales = Units Sold * Sale Price
- Sales (Net Sales) = Gross Sales - Discounts
- Profit = Sales - COGS
- Profit Margin = Profit / Sales
- Current Ratio = Total Assets / Liabilities
- Quick Ratio = (Total Assets - Inventory) / Liabilities
- Debt-to-Equity = Liabilities / Equity
- Return on Assets = Net Income / Total Assets
