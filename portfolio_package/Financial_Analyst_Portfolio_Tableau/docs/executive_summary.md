# Executive Summary - Sales and Profitability (Brew Portfolio)

## Objective
Help an analyst explain what is driving **Sales**, **Profit**, and **Margin** by Brand, Category, Retailer, and Channel - and quantify the tradeoff between discounting and profitability.

## Key insights to look for
- Which brands/categories have the healthiest gross profit margin?
- Does higher discount improve volume enough to increase total profit, or does it destroy margin?
- Are new products (New Product Tag) outperforming legacy products?

## Recommended decisions
- Set discount guardrails by category (max discount that still preserves target margin).
- Prioritize distribution into retailers/channels with consistently positive profit contribution.
- Review packaging and unit economics for products with high sales but low margin.
